# AIR SLASH physical-device playtest — pending

Automated checks do not establish real hands, motion blur, latency or delight.
Run five rounds each on a front-camera iPhone/Safari and Android/Chrome, plus
one desktop webcam. Record device/browser, inference FPS, tracking losses,
accidental cuts, first-slash time, score and the player's reaction.

1. Open/closed/relaxed hands all work. No finger pose is required. Verify the
   anatomical RIGHT / LEFT blade labels against the mirrored camera image.
2. First apple is readable and cuttable in two seconds. Confirm horizontal,
   vertical and diagonal cuts visibly match the actual hand sweep.
3. Still hands score nothing. Medium sweeps only leave trails. Fast/power
   thresholds feel distinct and attainable under motion blur.
4. Re-enter from an edge, cross arms, cover one hand and restore it. No jump
   cuts or explosive false hits; two blades maintain separate identities.
5. Lose both hands: clock and physics pause. Restore hands: stable recovery,
   180ms warm-up and a fresh intentional slash. Background/retry/exit releases
   camera, listeners, effects and audio.
6. Bomb appears after six seconds. Shake moves the camera scene; smoke and
   soot follow the face, −300 and combo reset occur, the round continues.
7. Five/ten combo, paired fruit, X-SLASH giant, bonus storm and final storm are
   satisfying. Confirm no recurring cuts from an old visual trail.
8. CREATOR: ORIGINAL/EFFECT/HIDE, best-event selection with reaction, seven-second
   replay, save/share native video and cancellation. HIDE never captures camera
   imagery. Video is silent. PLAY retains no recording.
9. Mobile short viewport / zoom / rotation, JA/EN, dual touch, mute, results,
   challenge sharing and clean retry are reachable without clipped controls.

Do not mark these items passed until a person plays with real camera input.
