# NOTE EATER v0.2 — device and human acceptance

Status: pending human testing. Synthetic browser checks are not acceptance results.
Spec: [EXP-016 v0.2](specs/EXP-016_NOTE_EATER_SPEC_v0.2.md).

Use Android Chrome and iPhone Safari through HTTPS. Choose NOTE EATER, allow
the front camera after PLAY, and test one face at a comfortable distance.
Do not explain the first bite with text or speech. Record device, browser,
light conditions, sound volume and source (camera/practice) with each session.

| Gate | Procedure | Required result | Actual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| False eat | Hold mouth closed for 30 seconds | Zero eats | Pending |
| Cold start | Five first-time players, no explanation | At least 4/5 first eats within five seconds of the first note | Pending |
| Recovery | Leave the frame, return, close and open mouth | Automatic recovery with no extra control | Pending |
