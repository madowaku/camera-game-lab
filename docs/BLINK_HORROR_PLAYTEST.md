# BLINK HORROR v0.2 physical playtest

Status: pending. Software checks and synthetic landmarks do not establish
physical input reliability or whether the game feels frightening/fair.

Run five rounds on Android Chrome and iOS Safari over HTTPS or localhost.
Record device, browser, camera, lighting/glasses, camera vs practice, escape/caught,
BLINKS/CLOSE CALLS/TIME, detection misses, pause/recovery and perceived difficulty.

- Does EXIT read as the objective immediately?
- Does the first blink teach “it gets closer” within ten seconds?
- Are nine of ten deliberate bilateral close/open cycles detected once each?
- Are winks ignored without preserving closed-eye defense?
- Is the visible locker obvious, and does BLINK NOW provide relief?
- Does DON'T LOOK clearly reverse the rule without an unfair first death?
- Is the GO audio cue audible and understandable with both eyes shut?
- Do face loss, darkness, glasses and returning to frame recover fairly?
- Does the final run feel like a 30–45s game, and does the player want to retry?
- Is the brief catch/final reflection readable without excessive loudness?

Mark results with actual human/device evidence. No fabricated pass counts.
